# Content Bible (Worlds 1–5)

## Structure
- World → Stage → Level → Micro-Activities
- Each level contains 3–5 micro-activities plus an end check.
- Every content node includes English + Hebrew fallback fields.

## Worlds Overview
### World 1: Foundations
- Focus: letter names, letter sounds, phoneme awareness, CVC blending.
- Stages:
  1. Alphabet Recognition
  2. Letter-Sound Mapping
  3. CVC Blending
  4. Short Vowel Focus

### World 2: Blending & Digraphs
- Focus: digraphs, consonant blends, word families.
- Stages:
  1. Consonant Blends (bl, st, tr)
  2. Digraphs (sh, ch, th)
  3. Word Families (-at, -in, -op)
  4. Mixed Review

### World 3: Vowel Teams
- Focus: long vowels, vowel teams, silent-e patterns.
- Stages:
  1. Silent-e (a_e, i_e, o_e)
  2. Vowel Teams (ai, ee, oa)
  3. R-controlled Vowels (ar, er, or)
  4. Mixed Review

### World 4: Multisyllabic Words
- Focus: syllable types, decoding longer words, prefixes/suffixes.
- Stages:
  1. Syllable Clapping & Segmentation
  2. Open/Closed Syllables
  3. Prefixes & Suffixes
  4. Compound Words

### World 5: Writing
- Focus: handwriting fluency, sentence construction, paragraph writing.
- Stages:
  1. Letter Formation & Spacing
  2. Word Building
  3. Sentence Mechanics (capitalization, punctuation)
  4. Paragraph Construction

## Level Structure
- Warmup (10–20 sec): quick recall.
- Core Activity 1: concept introduction.
- Core Activity 2: guided practice.
- Core Activity 3: independent practice.
- Exit Check: 5-item assessment.

## Review System
- Every 2 completed levels triggers a 5-item review in the next level.
- Every stage ends with a Stage Review (10 items).
- End-of-world Bridge Challenge pulls 15 items evenly across stage skills.

## Bridges
- Bridge Challenge gates the next world.
- 2/3 stars required; stars are awarded by accuracy and speed.
- Failure sends the player to a focused review path of 2 levels.

## Endgame Paragraph
- Final activity of World 5 is a 4–6 sentence paragraph about a personal experience.
- Must include capitalization, punctuation, and at least 4 targeted vocabulary words.
- Scored on structure, spelling accuracy, and completeness.

## No Gaps Policy
- Each stage includes a cumulative review of prior stages.
- Skills never disappear; they return with spaced intervals.
- Any skill under mastery threshold is resurfaced within the next two levels.
